## Selected work

**[QuantX](https://reinhardtweyers.com/projects/quantx)**<br>
An AI trading app for stocks, on the web and as an installable phone app.
It has 2,000 users.
Over US$530,000 of user capital trades through its agent.<br>
`React` `Node.js` `Anthropic API` `Solidity` `MySQL`

**[Caskt](https://reinhardtweyers.com/projects/caskt)**<br>
A free desktop app for managing Counter-Strike 2 inventories.
It has over 2,700 active installs.
The [source code](https://github.com/0xRetroDev/caskt) is public.<br>
`TypeScript` `Electron` `React` `SQLite`

**[Everroad](https://reinhardtweyers.com/projects/everroad)**<br>
An open-world driving game with a world that is generated as you drive.
It went from first commit to a playtest build in under three weeks.<br>
`three.js` `JavaScript` `Electron` `Steamworks`
